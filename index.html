07Questions

Questions, answered.

Short answers about languages, your data and what this website does.

What does Rudra24 AI bring together?

Lead research, your AI assistant, recruitment, CRM, exports and outreach preparation. Connected accounts and providers enable additional workflows.

Can I use Hindi and English?

The assistant includes Hindi and English voice workflows. Voice availability depends on the selected provider, microphone permissions and account setup.

Does this website make calls or send messages?

No. The interactive previews use fictional examples. Real calling and messaging happen in the app after the required provider and account setup.
