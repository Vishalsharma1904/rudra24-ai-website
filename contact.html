Questions

Questions, answered.

Short answers about languages, your data and what this website does.

Can I see the app before deciding?

Ask for a product walkthrough on WhatsApp. Tell us your business type and the features you want to see.

What should I share about my business?

Your industry, the locations you cover and the tasks your team wants to simplify are a useful starting point. Do not send passwords or API keys.

How does pricing work?

Contact the team for the current product offer. Third-party AI, calling and messaging providers may have separate plans or usage charges.

What is needed for calling and account integrations?

It depends on the workflow. Calling needs backend and telephony setup; email, calendar, Sheets and WhatsApp need their respective authorised accounts or provider configuration.
